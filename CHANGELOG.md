## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-03

First release.

### Added
- 19 games: Indian classics, international board games and arcade games, with single player, pass-and-play and Wi-Fi multiplayer
- Local-network multiplayer: the host iPhone is the server (Bonjour discovery, join by address, auto-reconnect, reactions)
- Generated SVG avatars with an editor and SVG export
- How to play: step-by-step guide and a YouTube tutorial link for every game, in the app and in docs/how-to-play.md
- Hourly background reinstall for free Apple teams (LaunchAgent), same as CineFlix
