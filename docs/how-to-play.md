# How to play

Every game also has a **How to play** screen inside the app: open a game from **Play**, or tap the **?** button while playing.
The video links below open a YouTube search for that game, so you always get current tutorials rather than one fixed video.

**Playing together:** one iPhone taps **Host a room**, the other opens **Nearby** and joins. Both need the same Wi-Fi or a hotspot.
For turn-based games you can also choose **Pass & play** on a single phone, or **Play vs computer** at Easy, Medium or Hard.

## Indian classics

### Ludo (2–4 players) — [watch how to play](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+play+ludo)
1. Roll the dice. You need a **6** to bring a token out of its yard.
2. Move a token clockwise round the track by the number rolled. Tap a glowing token to choose it (a single possible move is played for you).
3. Landing on an opponent's token sends it back to its yard, unless it is on a **star** or a coloured start square.
4. A 6, a capture or reaching home gives another roll. Three 6s in a row lose the turn.
5. Take all four tokens up your coloured column into the centre. You need the exact number to enter. First to finish wins.

### Snakes & Ladders (2–4 players) — [watch how to play](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+play+snakes+and+ladders)
1. Tap **Roll dice** and move that many squares from square 1 towards 100.
2. Land at the bottom of a ladder to climb it. Land on a snake's head and you slide down to its tail.
3. You must roll the exact number to land on 100. A 6 gives another roll; three 6s in a row lose the move.

### Carrom (2 players) — [watch how to play](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+play+carrom)
1. Slide the striker along your baseline, then drag back from it to aim and release to shoot.
2. Pocket your own colour (white or black). Pocketing your own piece gives another shot.
3. The queen scores more but must be "covered" by pocketing one of your own pieces straight after.
4. Pocketing the striker is a foul. Clear all your pieces to win.

### Mancala (2 players) — [watch how to play](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+play+mancala+kalah)
1. Tap one of your pits (the bottom row). Its seeds are sown one by one counter-clockwise, including your store (the big pit on your right) but skipping your opponent's.
2. If your last seed lands in your store, play again.
3. If it lands in an empty pit of yours, you capture it and the seeds in the pit opposite.
4. When one side is empty, the other player keeps what is left. Most seeds in the store wins.

### Goats & Tigers — Bagh-Chal (2 players) — [watch how to play](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+play+bagh+chal)
1. The first player is the **goats** (20 of them), the second is the **tigers** (4, starting in the corners). Goats move first.
2. Goats are placed one per turn on any empty point. After all 20 are placed they slide along the lines.
3. Tigers slide one step, or **jump** over an adjacent goat along a line into an empty point to capture it.
4. Tigers win by capturing 5 goats. Goats win by trapping every tiger so none can move.

## Board and strategy

### Chess (2 players) — [watch how to play](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+play+chess+for+beginners)
1. Tap a piece to see where it can go, then tap a square, or drag the piece. Green dots are moves, rings are captures.
2. Special moves: castling (move the king two squares towards a rook), en passant, and pawn promotion (a picker appears).
3. Checkmate wins. Stalemate, insufficient material, the fifty-move rule and threefold repetition are draws. **Resign** is under the board.

### Checkers (2 players) — [watch how to play](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+play+checkers+draughts)
1. Move diagonally forward one square. Tap a piece, then a highlighted square.
2. If you can jump an opponent's piece you **must**; keep jumping while you can (the piece stays selected).
3. Reach the far row to become a king, which moves and jumps in all four diagonal directions.
4. You win when your opponent has no pieces or no legal move.

### Reversi (2 players) — [watch how to play](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+play+reversi+othello)
1. Place a disc on a dotted square so it traps a straight line of your opponent's discs between your new disc and another of yours.
2. All trapped discs flip to your colour. If you have no legal move you pass automatically.
3. When neither player can move, the player with more discs wins. Corners are very valuable.

### Connect Four (2 players) — [watch how to play](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+play+connect+four)
1. Tap a column to drop your disc to the lowest free slot.
2. Be first to line up four discs horizontally, vertically or diagonally. A full board is a draw.

### Gomoku (2 players) — [watch how to play](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+play+gomoku)
1. Tap an intersection (or drag; the stone appears above your finger so it isn't hidden), then press **Place stone**.
2. Make an unbroken line of five or more stones in any direction. Black moves first.
3. Block your opponent's open threes and fours, and try to build two threats at once.

### Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe (2 players) — [watch how to play](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+play+ultimate+tic+tac+toe)
1. Each big square holds a small tic-tac-toe board. Where you play inside a small board decides which small board your opponent must play in next (it glows).
2. Win a small board with three in a row. If you are sent to a board that is already decided, you may play anywhere.
3. Win three small boards in a row to win the game.

### Dots & Boxes (2–4 players) — [watch how to play](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+play+dots+and+boxes)
1. Tap or drag near a gap between two dots to draw a line. A preview shows which line you will draw.
2. Drawing the fourth side of a box claims it for you and gives you another turn.
3. Avoid drawing a box's third side: it lets your opponent take it. Most boxes wins.

## Arcade

### Memory Match (1–4 players) — [watch how to play](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+play+memory+matching+game)
1. Tap two cards. A match is kept and you go again; a mismatch flips back and the next player goes.
2. Alone, finish in as few tries as you can.

### Air Hockey (2 players) — [watch how to play](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+play+air+hockey)
1. Drag your mallet to hit the puck into your opponent's goal. First to 7 wins.
2. On two phones, each player controls their own mallet on their own screen.

### 2048 (1 player) — [watch how to play](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+play+2048)
1. Swipe to slide all tiles. Equal tiles that collide merge into one with the sum.
2. Reach 2048 to win, then keep going. **Undo** steps back up to 30 moves. Pick 4×4, 5×5 or 6×6 from the menu.

### Snake (1 player) — [watch how to play](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+play+snake+game)
1. Swipe anywhere to steer; you can queue two quick turns.
2. Eat the food to grow. Classic: walls kill. Wrap: edges wrap round. Speed: faster and faster. Never touch yourself.

### Blocks (1 player) — [watch how to play](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+play+tetris)
1. Drag sideways to move a piece, **tap to rotate**, drag down to soft drop, flick down to hard drop, swipe up to hold. The buttons below do the same.
2. Fill a whole row to clear it. Clearing four rows at once scores most. The game speeds up every ten lines.

### Minesweeper (1 player) — [watch how to play](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+play+minesweeper)
1. Tap a square to reveal it. A number shows how many of the 8 neighbours are mines.
2. Long-press (or use the flag button) to flag a suspected mine. Tap a satisfied number to reveal its remaining neighbours.
3. Reveal every safe square to win. Your first tap is always safe.

### Sudoku (1 player) — [watch how to play](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=how+to+play+sudoku+for+beginners)
1. Fill the grid so every row, column and 3×3 box contains 1 to 9 once.
2. Select a cell and tap a number. Turn on **Notes** to pencil in candidates. Conflicts are highlighted.
3. Stuck? Use **Hint**. Every puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved by logic.
